Garena Free Fire is a highly popular mobile battle royale game developed and published by Garena. Each fast-paced match drops 50 players onto a remote island where they must search for weapons and resources to survive. The ultimate goal is to outlast all opponents within a shrinking safe zone to secure a victory known as a "Booyah".

The game stands out for its high accessibility, as it is optimized to run smoothly on lower-end smartphones with limited RAM. It features a diverse roster of characters, each possessing unique skills and abilities that add tactical depth to the gameplay. This accessibility and deep customization have helped it break records, making it one of the most downloaded mobile games globally with massive competitive esports tournaments.
