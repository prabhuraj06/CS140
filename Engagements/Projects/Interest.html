KTM RC-390

The KTM RC 390 is a lightweight, single-cylinder sport bike engineered for riders who prioritize sharp handling and a race-focused experience. Driven by its signature "Ready to Race" philosophy, the bike pairs a 373cc liquid-cooled engine with a nimble trellis frame, delivering roughly 44 horsepower and 37 Nm of torque. While its aggressive ergonomics and firm seat make it less suited for long-distance commuting or highway cruising, it excels as a track-day weapon and canyon carver.

Royal Enfield

Royal Enfield is the world’s oldest motorcycle brand in continuous production, famous for its vintage-inspired cruisers and mid-sized engines. Operating under the philosophy of "Pure Motorcycling," the company prioritizes timeless retro aesthetics, accessible torque, and relaxed riding dynamics over raw high-speed performance. Their bikes rely heavily on robust, low-maintenance air-cooled or air-oil-cooled single and twin-cylinder engines that deliver a distinctive, rhythmic exhaust note affectionately known by fans as the "thump."
